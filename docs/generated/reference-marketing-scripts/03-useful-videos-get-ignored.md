## Transcript

If your useful videos are getting ignored, the lesson may be arriving before the reason to care. A lot of experts open with background, definitions, and context because they want every sentence to be accurate. But the viewer has not felt the problem yet, so every correct sentence starts to sound like homework. Start with a moment they recognize from their own day, then teach the fix. First, name the frustrating moment in plain language. Next, show the difference between the weak version and the stronger version. Then, give one small action the viewer can try today. For example, do not open with three principles of follow-up. Open with: your lead did not disappear; your follow-up gave them nothing easy to answer. Then show the better message. The value is still the lesson. The hook simply gives the viewer a reason to stay long enough to receive it. Use this structure on your next script, then save the result beside the old version and compare them.
