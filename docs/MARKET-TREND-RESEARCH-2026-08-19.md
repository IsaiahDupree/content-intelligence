Snapshot time: 2026-08-19 02:18 EDT for the Google Trends demand capture. Social observations were collected between 01:24 and 03:03 EDT.

This report is market-led. No business, AI, automation, or creator-economy topic constraint was applied.

## Scope

- Google Trends displayed 477 active United States searches. The first 100 rows were reviewed.
- 74 distinct Google trend queries were expanded with signed-in YouTube search.
- A keyword-independent YouTube `mostPopular` sweep collected 629 videos across 13 working categories.
- The first Google query wave added 120 deduplicated YouTube videos.
- The rows 26-100 expansion processed 191 records and added 184 unique videos. Five additional Blue Jays/Rays rows were recovered from a partial extractor result.
- A timeout retry added three Pete Hegseth videos; Elissa Slotkin and Oliver Larkin remained measured timeouts after two attempts.
- An adaptive second iteration tested 10 phrases derived from observed videos, not from a static topic list. It processed 47 records and added 35 unique videos.
- A third iteration tested six narrower gaming, combat-sports, and entertainment phrases. It processed 24 records and added 14 unique videos.
- A fourth breadth iteration tested six movie, sports, music, consumer-finance, and celebrity phrases. It processed 30 records and added 21 unique videos.
- Controlled-browser evidence covered X, Threads, Instagram, and TikTok. Facebook produced no usable current evidence in this cycle.
- Across the Google and adaptive waves, 96 current-query attempts were measured. Empty, weak, concentrated, partial, and timed-out results remain in the receipts instead of being hidden.

Search demand is not video reach. Google volume, cumulative platform views, implied views per hour, creator breadth, and concentration are reported separately.

## Operating Decision

The production collector should continue this loop without a fixed niche restriction:

```text
provider charts and current search demand
  -> exact-query expansion
  -> immutable observations
  -> velocity, breadth, and concentration ranking
  -> derived phrase/hashtag frontier
  -> bounded adaptive iterations across both winners and unrelated sectors
  -> promote useful queries; cool empty or concentrated queries
  -> repeat
```

Broad chart exploration must remain enabled. It found Roblox, Minecraft, gaming, pets, and format-level signals that a static keyword list would not have discovered. Exact-query capacity should favor measured winners such as GTA gameplay leaks and 2K27 subtopics, while a fixed exploration share continues scanning unrelated sectors.
